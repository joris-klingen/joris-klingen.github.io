Hi, I'm Joris

Senior Data Scientist at the City of Amsterdam and researcher affiliated with Spatial Economics at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam.

Background

I hold a PhD from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, where I was supervised by Jos van Ommeren and Erik Verhoef. My research focuses on empirical applications in transport economics and environmental economics — including road safety, immediate effects of air pollution, and urban cycling.

Before my PhD, I studied Economics and Econometrics at Tinbergen Institute, Spatial, Transport and Environmental Economics at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, and Future Planet Studies at Universiteit van Amsterdam. In 2019, I visited Imperial College London, where I worked with Dan Graham at the Transport Strategy Centre.

Research Interests

Environmental economics · Transport economics · Urban economics · Applied econometrics
