Research

My work focuses on empirical applications in transport economics and environmental economics, using natural experiments and causal inference methods.

PhD Thesis

Natural Experiments in Environmental and Transport Economics

Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, 2021 · Supervisors: Jos van Ommeren & Erik Verhoef

Five chapters exploring natural experiments across environmental and transport economics: air pollution and decision-making in chess, ozone effects on cycling speed, public bicycle demand during metro interruptions, bike lane effects on traffic and safety in NYC, and smartphone distraction in road accidents.
